About me

I am a first-year undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Business Administration in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship (DBE) at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB), alongside preparing for my Chartered Accountancy (CA Foundation) examinations. But beyond the ledgers and lecture halls, I bring a lively, curious energy to everything I do. Whether I'm hosting an event, sharing a story that brings a room to life, or lightening the mood with a quick laugh, I love turning ordinary moments into engaging experiences.

Operating at the unique intersection of rigorous quantitative discipline and creative execution, I combine financial logic with a natural intuition for people. Whether I am managing high-volume operations for community fundraisers, building economic 3D models, or shaping digital stories through photography, video editing, and Canva, I approach every project with sharp focus and genuine warmth.

Away from all this, you will find me swimming, sketching, or exploring philosophy. For me, life is an ongoing, multidisciplinary journey—one defined by boundless curiosity, shared laughter, and the unforgettable memories made along the way.
